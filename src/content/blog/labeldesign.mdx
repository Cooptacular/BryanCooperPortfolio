# Sanctuary Farm Wines
![Wine Label](../../assets/graphic-design/SanctuaryFarmWines-Heron-Cab.png)

My Grandpa, The Chemist, is also a Master Winemaker. He sources his grapes and juice from Northeast Ohio, and makes wine in the basement of his log cabin home. 
There are few things I enjoy more than sitting with him and my family on his front porch listening to the birds sing, and having great conversation with a glass of Sanctuary Farm Wines in hand. 
I've had fun designing labels and practicing my print design on his wines. 
The Great Blue Heron can often be seen stalking through the reeds around his lake, as well as my childhood home lake, so the Heron is a recurring character in my designs. More on the heron in a separate blog post though...

# Wedding Beer Label
![Beer Label](../../assets/graphic-design/Wedding-BeerLabel-CardDesign.png)

For my wedding, I collaborated with my friends over at The Crooked Pecker, the greatest brewery on Earth mind you, and designed these custom beer labels. 
I designed the label artwork, and they got them custom printed and white-labelled one of their delicious hazy IPAs which we served at our wedding reception.
It was a bucket list goal of mine to design a beer label, and I had a blast creating a playing card design. 
The concept for the playing card was sitting in a sketchbook for years, and it was fitting to bring it to life for the best day of my life.
